Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Run, jump and slide away from the goofy fuzzy monster neighbor!

About the game

The kid runs by himself through the neighborhood while the fuzzy monster next door chases him for a hug. Tap to jump over garden gnomes and fences, swipe down to slide under laundry lines, bounce on trampolines, double jump over boxes and dodge balls rolling at you. Every trip lets the monster get closer, and if he catches you it is a big silly hug and back to the last flag. Grab all 3 keys, then hide and watch the monster run right past you. There are 100 levels in 5 worlds with a boss level every 10 levels.

  • 100 running levels in 5 neighborhood worlds
  • Jump, slide, bounce and double jump to escape
  • Goofy fuzzy monster neighbor, funny and never scary
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
