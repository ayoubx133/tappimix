About the game

Each event is a match told in quick highlight chances: tap when the target is green to shoot, tap a free teammate to pass, curl free kicks over the wall, dive to save shots and smash volleys. Win events to collect new player cards from bronze to legend, and spend coins to upgrade your best stars. A stronger team makes every chance easier. Play 100 events in 5 cups and unlock 6 colorful balls.

  • 100 events in 5 cups from bronze to legend
  • Collect 100 original player cards and upgrade them
  • Shots, passes, free kicks, saves and volleys
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
