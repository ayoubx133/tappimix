About the game

Pull back on the silly calico cat and let go to fling it across the room like a bouncy ball. Smash vases, splat sauce bottles, topple toy towers, ring bells and pop balloons. Hit lots of things quickly for big combos and fill the star bar before your flings run out. When the owner comes home, the cat just puts on a halo and whistles! 100 levels in 5 worlds with giant boss treats to smash.
