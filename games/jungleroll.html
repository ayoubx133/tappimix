About the game

Your jungle ball rolls forward across wooden rope bridges high above a turquoise river. Swipe left and right to switch lanes around missing planks, swipe up to jump over logs and gaps, dodge swinging logs and bounce from hippo to hippo. Collect bananas, open surprise crates and race a cheeky monkey every 10 levels. 100 levels in 5 worlds with 6 balls to unlock.

  • 100 levels in 5 jungle worlds with new obstacles in each
  • Silly splashes, hippo hops, toucan rides and monkey races
  • 6 jungle balls to unlock with bananas and coins
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
