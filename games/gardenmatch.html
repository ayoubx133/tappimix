Royal Garden Swap

FamilyFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Swap garden gems, pull silly weeds and help Rosie bloom her palace garden!

About the game

Swap two neighbouring garden gems to line up three or more of the same kind and finish the goals on the garden sign before the moves run out. Match four, five, an L shape or a square to grow garden hoses, flower bombs, rainbow butterflies and helpful ladybugs, then swap two boosters together for giant combos. Pull out goofy weeds, splash away puddles, send buzzy bees home, tickle sleepy moles and plant seed pots across 100 levels in 5 worlds, with a grumpy giant weed to cheer away every 10th level. Every win blooms a new hedge, arch, fountain or bench in Rosie's garden.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds with a giant weed boss every 10th level
  • Garden hoses, flower bombs, butterflies and ladybug boosters
  • Restore a royal palace garden and dress Rosie in 6 outfits
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
