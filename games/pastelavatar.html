My Pastel Town Avatar

FamilyFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Make your artist avatar and paint a cute pastel town in your own colors!

About the game

Pick a color from the paint palette and tap anything to paint it: walls, lamps, cupcakes, the puppy's spots, nails in the spa and the grey sketch houses in town. When a house is fully painted it comes alive and a new animal neighbor pops out to say hello. Doodle on the easel, change your hair, hats and outfits, frost cupcakes for animal friends, play fetch with your puppy and decorate a dream house with furniture you can move and paint. Finish 55 quests and daily tasks to unlock places, earn coins and fill your sticker book.

  • Paint everything with a 10 color pastel palette
  • 6 places: studio, cafe, puppy park, spa, sketch town, dream house
  • Avatar maker, 55 quests, daily tasks and a 60 sticker book
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
