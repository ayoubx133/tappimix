About the game

Help a sneaky tuxedo kitty escape the house one hop at a time. Tap a glowing paw to hop onto tables, shelves and even grandpa's head, grab the golden key and slip out of the cat flap. Every hop is one tick, and grandpa peeks every few hops, so hide in a box, behind the curtain or under the table before his eye opens! Watch out for the tattle parrot, the robo vacuum and squeaky spots across 100 levels in 5 worlds.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds with new tricks in every world
  • A sleepy grandpa, a tattle parrot and a robo vacuum
  • Undo, wait and hint buttons so kids never get stuck
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
