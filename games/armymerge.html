About the game

Tap BUY to add toy soldiers to your merge grid, then drag two of the same toy together to merge them into a stronger one. Your toy army fires foam darts at the grumpy goo blobs marching toward your block wall. Shoot the bonus gates to turn their number positive for extra gems, and keep merging up to jeeps, tanks, choppers and robots. Defend 100 waves in 5 worlds with bubble shield goo, splitting goo, speedy goo and big goo bosses.

  • 100 waves in 5 worlds, each with a new goo twist
  • Merge 8 toy units from rookie to star titan
  • Upgrade your wall and collect 6 toy army skins
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
