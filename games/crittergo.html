About the game

Walk through cozy maps and tap the rustling bushes to meet cute critters. Flick the bubble ball up when the ring is small for a super catch, but watch out for wind, shy critters and bubble shields. Every catch gives candy so your baby critters can grow up, and every new friend fills a page of your collection book. Find all 100 original critters across 100 levels in 5 worlds.

  • 100 original critters to collect and evolve
  • 100 levels in 5 worlds from meadow to rainbow peaks
  • Simple flick to catch, perfect for kids
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
