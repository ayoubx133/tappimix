About the game

Drag your town friends into the house, the hair salon, the bakery, the park, the school and the pet shop. Cook yummy meals, bake and decorate cupcakes, try new hairstyles and outfits, and put friends to bed when it gets late. There are no rules: tap everything to see what happens, plant flowers, adopt pets and decorate the rooms your way. Finish fun quests and daily tasks to earn coins, collect stickers and unlock new places, friends and styles.

  • 9 places to explore: house, salon, bakery, boutique, park, school and pet shop
  • 8 friends to dress up, feed, style and move anywhere you like
  • 90 quests, daily tasks, stickers and new places to unlock
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
