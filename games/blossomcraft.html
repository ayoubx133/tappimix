Blossom Craft World

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Grow a cozy blossom garden, build a block house and enjoy four seasons!

About the game

Pick a seed and tap a garden bed to plant it, then drag the watering can over your plants and watch them sprout, bud and bloom with happy smiling faces. Tap the flowers to make them sing and pick them for petals. Use petals and coins to grow your tent into a blossom villa and decorate with lanterns, a koi pond, a swing, a bridge and more. Every few flowers the season changes from spring to summer, autumn and winter, with new seeds and silly visitors like a mole, a sneaky raccoon and a hat wearing frog.

  • Plant, water and pick 9 kinds of singing flowers
  • Four seasons, a growing house and 10 decorations
  • 50 quests, daily tasks and silly garden critters
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
