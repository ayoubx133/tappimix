About the game

Every tap is one tiptoe step, then the family takes a turn. Wait while dad snores, step around creaky boards, dodge mom on her walk and throw a squeaky duck to make them look away. Grab your sneakers and backpack, hide in blanket forts and reach the door without being seen. Finish in few steps to earn all three stars across 100 levels in 5 worlds.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds with new family tricks
  • Turn based sneaking puzzles anyone can play
  • Hide, distract and pack your bag to escape
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
