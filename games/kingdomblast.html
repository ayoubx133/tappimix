Kingdom Blast Quest

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Blast royal blocks, fire rockets and help the princess rebuild her kingdom!

About the game

Tap a group of 2 or more touching blocks of the same color to blast them and reach the level goals before the moves run out. Blast 5 or more at once to make a rocket, 7 or more for a firework bomb and 9 or more for a magic rainbow orb, then tap two specials side by side for a giant combo. Every win gives a royal star that the princess uses to rebuild her kingdom, from the Meadow Gate to the Crystal Palace. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds with barrels, dirt patches, magic vines, treasure chests and floating barrels.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds with a new challenge in every world
  • Rebuild 5 kingdom areas with the stars you win
  • Rockets, firework bombs and rainbow orb combos
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
