Help a little fox build a floating block island for happy villagers and pets!

About the game

Tap trees, rocks, berry bushes and flowers and watch your fox builder bonk out wood, stone, berries and petals. Pick a building, tap a glowing spot and blocks rain from the sky to stack up into houses, farms, bakeries, windmills, treehouses, lighthouses and even a castle. Villagers fly in by hot air balloon and wish for new buildings, and every wish you grant makes them dance. Grow the island with new land, adopt pets, collect coins and finish 52 quests and daily tasks.

  • Build 12 blocky buildings with falling block magic
  • Villagers, pets, day and night and silly surprises
  • 52 quests, daily tasks and 24 stickers
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
