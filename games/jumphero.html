About the game

Help the fox hero run and jump through bright blocky worlds full of coins. Use the left and right pads to run and the jump pad to leap over gaps, hold it longer to jump higher. Bounce on top of friendly jelly blobs, ride moving platforms, spring off bouncy pads and dash across crumbly bridges before they fall. Find the 3 hidden star coins in every level and reach the flag to finish.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds, each with a brand new trick
  • Classic run and jump controls with hearts and checkpoints
  • Collect star coins and unlock 6 colorful heroes
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
