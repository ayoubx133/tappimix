Prank the Teacher

Set up silly pranks in a grumpy teacher's house without being seen!

About the game

Press and hold a prank spot to set up a whoopee cushion, a slime bucket, a glue hat, googly eyes and more. Let go fast when the grumpy teacher lowers his newspaper or turns around. Watch out for his tattletale parrot, keep the noise meter low and stay out of the flashlight at night. When every prank is ready, enjoy the funny surprise show across 100 levels in 5 worlds.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds with new rooms and tricks
  • Hold and let go timing that is easy to learn
  • Silly pranks like slime, confetti and googly eyes
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
