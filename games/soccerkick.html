About the game

Drag on the left to run with your player and tap PASS or SHOOT to attack, or STEAL and DASH to win the ball back. Every match is a quick arcade game against a cartoon CPU team with its own kit, crest and formation. Win league matches and cup finals across 100 matches in 5 leagues, from a sunny park to snowy pitches and windy star nights. Earn coins and unlock 6 colorful team kits.

  • 100 matches in 5 leagues with cup finals
  • Easy joystick plus pass, shoot, steal and super shots
  • Snow, wind and night stadiums with 6 team kits
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
