Block Builder Craft

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Build a block town, welcome villagers and finish fun building quests.

About the game

Pick a building card and tap a free lot: houses, bakeries, parks and toy shops pop up block by block. Villagers move in and walk the streets, and your shops fill bubbles with bricks that you tap to collect and spend on new buildings. Finish 100 build quests in 5 towns: build piers on the harbor water, tap snow off the roofs, dig wells to water desert farms and ride balloon lifts up to the cloud islands.

  • 100 build quests in 5 towns with a new twist in each
  • Villagers move in and your town grows block by block
  • Tap brick bubbles to earn and keep building
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
