About the game

Tap anywhere and your hero walks there, digs through blocks and grabs the loot. Find treasure chests, collect shiny gems, bring apples to hungry villagers and pop bouncy slimes before the sun goes down. Explore dark glow caves, slide on slippery ice and unlock temple doors with keys. Play 100 quests in 5 worlds with a Slime King and a treasure vault every 10 levels.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds of block islands
  • Silly slimes, surprise critters and treasure chests
  • 6 heroes to unlock with your coins
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
