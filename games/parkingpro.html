About the game

Turn the steering wheel with one finger and hold GO or BACK with the other to drive your car into the glowing parking spot. Squeeze between parked cars, parallel park on city streets, wait for a gap in moving traffic and tow a trailer into its bay. Every bump costs a star, so drive slowly and carefully. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds and visit the garage to paint and tune your cars.

  • 100 parking levels in 5 worlds: lots, streets, traffic, trailers and night mall
  • Real steering wheel and pedals with smooth top down driving
  • Garage with 8 paints, stripes, spoilers, neon glow and 6 cars
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
