About the game

Tap the stone pads to build bubble towers, pillow catapults and wind fans along the winding path, then upgrade them to stop the silly goo blobs before they reach the castle gate. When the Queen is ready, tap her button and she jumps in with a giant pillow slam. Watch out for fast hoppers, helmet goo, balloon goo that flies and goo that splits in two. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds with a giant goo boss every 10 levels.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds with a giant goo boss every 10 levels
  • Bubble towers, pillow catapults, wind fans and a Queen pillow slam
  • 6 castle colours to unlock
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
