About the game

Drag left and right to steer your squad down the desert road while everyone sprays water automatically at the silly green goo blobs. Pick the best gates for more buddies and faster spray, and splash the red gates until they turn blue. Choose upgrade cards like Rapid Spray or Kitty Power, dodge tumbleweeds and pop goo nests. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds and beat the giant Goo King every 10 levels.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds with a Goo King boss every 10 levels
  • Water blaster squad, upgrade cards and a pouncing kitty
  • 6 blaster colours to unlock
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
