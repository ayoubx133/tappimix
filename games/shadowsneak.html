About the game

Tap the floor to move your ninja kid and stay out of the flashlight beams. Sneak up behind each sleepy guard and tag him with your magic wand so he falls asleep with a big Zzz. Grab the shiny gems for three stars, hide in bushes, dodge cameras, find keys and slip past spinning lanterns. There are 100 levels in 5 worlds to explore.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds with a new trick in each world
  • Simple tap to move stealth that kids love
  • Six fun ninja outfits to unlock with coins
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
