About the game

Play as a fluffy kitty with a blue bow tie in grandma's cozy houses. Tap the floor to walk and tap things to be naughty: knock over vases, steal the fish, scratch curtains, play the piano and nap in sunny spots. When grandma sees you she comes running with her feather duster, so hide in a box or climb up high, or get tickled! Finish 50 funny quests and daily tasks to rank up, unlock 5 houses, hats and kitty coats.

  • 5 houses with 15 rooms full of silly things to do
  • 50 quests, daily tasks, ranks and cute hats
  • A funny grandma who chases, slips, naps and tickles
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
