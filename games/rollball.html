About the game

Drag left and right to steer your rolling ball along wooden tracks high above the clouds. Jump over gaps, dodge sliding blocks and swinging foam hammers, ride the wind and zoom on turbo pads. Grab coins and star gems, open surprise gifts and finish with a giant bowling strike every 10 levels. 100 levels in 5 worlds with 6 fun balls to unlock.

  • 100 levels in 5 sky worlds with new obstacles in each
  • Funny ball faces, umbrella falls and bowling strike finales
  • 6 cool balls to unlock with coins
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
