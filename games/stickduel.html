Stick Duel Legends

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Wobbly stickman duels with foam swords and pillows, solo or with a friend!

About the game

Hold the arrow pads to move your wobbly stickman and tap SWING to swoosh your foam sword, pillow or noodle. Bonk the other fighter until they flop down seeing stars, then watch them pop right back up for the next round. Win 100 duels against silly CPU fighters in 5 arenas with jumpy blocks, trampolines, slippy ice and moving clouds. Or pick 2 PLAYERS and battle a friend on the same tablet.

  • 100 CPU duels in 5 arenas with new tricks in every arena
  • 2 players on one tablet with their own pads
  • Floppy ragdoll physics with foam swords, pillows and noodles
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
