About the game

Your smiling jelly cube with big headphones dashes forward to a pumping synth beat, and every bump, pit and block is placed on the music. Tap or hold to jump, bounce off yellow pads, tap glowing rings in mid air, flip gravity to run on the ceiling and fly a little rocket through block walls. Checkpoints bring you right back after every bonk, so anyone can reach the finish. Clear a level without a single bonk for three stars and earn coins for new cubes.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds, each with its own song and a new move
  • One tap controls with friendly checkpoints
  • Unlock 6 colorful cubes with the coins you earn
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
