About the game

Walk around a sunny school yard full of blossom trees, chat with classmates, ring the school bell and see what silly thing happens next. Play 7 classes as quick mini games: pop math bubbles, paint pictures, jump the rope, tap the music notes, mix colors in science, pack a lunch box and copy dance moves in the club. Dress up in the closet with hairstyles, colors, uniforms and funny extras, then visit the town cafe, adopt a pet, win plushies and take photos with friends. Finish 60 quests and daily tasks to collect stickers and grade up.

  • 7 funny class mini games plus a dance club
  • 60 quests, daily tasks and 24 stickers to collect
  • Closet, pets, cafe treats, claw game and photo booth
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
