Grumpy Grandpa Farm Escape

FamilyFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Dash between hay bales, grab the gate key and sneak off grumpy grandpa's farm!

About the game

Tap a hay bale, barrel or pumpkin pile to dash to it and hide while grumpy grandpa sweeps his lantern around the farmyard. Grab the golden key, slip past his light and run out through the farm gate. Moo the cow to distract him, tiptoe past the napping goose and dodge Biscuit the dog on his tractor. If grandpa spots you he just shouts GOTCHA and sends you back to the hay cart, so try again! There are 100 levels in 5 worlds with a boss level every 10 levels.

  • 100 levels in 5 farm worlds, each with a new trick
  • Hide in hay, distract grandpa with silly animals
  • Funny grumpy grandpa, cute and never scary
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
