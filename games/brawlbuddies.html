About the game

Lead Splash, the cheeky masked hero in a red bandana and purple hoodie, or one of 9 other buddies. Drag anywhere to move around the arena while your buddy splashes the pink team automatically with water balloons, foam punches and bubbles. Grab gems from the middle, hold the capture zone or win a splash tag race together with two bot teammates. When the gadget pad lights up, tap it for a big balloon, a foam dash or a bubble shield. Play 100 matches in 5 worlds on the trophy road and unlock 10 different buddies.

  • 100 matches in 5 worlds with gem grab, capture zone and splash tag
  • 10 unlockable buddies with soft gadgets and fun hats
  • Kid friendly splash battles: buddies just get splatted and bounce back
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
