About the game

Run across a giant candy keyboard where every key you step on plays a note and gives you plus one speed. Tap to jump over candy blocks and gaps, bounce on soda springs, double jump and swipe down to slide under lollipop bars. A silly pink goo blob is chasing you because it just wants a hug, so keep your speed up and slam the exit door before it catches you. 100 levels in 5 worlds with 6 heroes to unlock.

  • 100 levels in 5 candy worlds with new moves in each
  • Plus one speed on every step and a funny goo blob chaser
  • 6 heroes to unlock with candy coins
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
