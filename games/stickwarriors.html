About the game

Your stickman hero stands in the middle with a glowing aura. Tap the left or right side of the screen to dash and bonk the goofy goo blobs coming from that side, and build combos to fill your aura meter. When it is full you go SUPER AURA and every tap throws a shining energy ball. Fight 100 levels in 5 worlds: Crystal Cliffs, Sky Temple, Thunder Clouds, Sunset Volcano and Space Arena, with flying jelly bats, helmet bots, pie throwers, teleporting blobs and a big boss every 10th fight.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds with a new silly foe in every world
  • Super Aura mode with energy balls and big combos
  • 6 glowing aura styles and boss fights
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
