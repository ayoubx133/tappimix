Tap glowing music tiles in rhythm while a cute kitty hops and dances along!

About the game

Music tiles slide down a glowing stage and every tile is a note of the song. Tap each tile when it reaches the line and your kitty hops onto it and plays the tune. Hold the long bubble tiles, swipe the arrow tiles and catch the sliding tiles as you travel through 100 levels in 5 worlds. Win dance-offs against silly rivals, collect coins and unlock 6 adorable kitties.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds, each song with its own melody
  • Funny kitty reactions, surprise events and dance-off rivals
  • 6 cute kitties to unlock with coins
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
