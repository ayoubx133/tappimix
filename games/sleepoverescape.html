Sneaky Sleepover Escape

FamilyFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Tiptoe past snoring parents and sneak out to your best friend's sleepover!

About the game

Hold the screen to tiptoe through the sleepy house and let go to freeze when dad stops snoring. If he peeks while you stand still he thinks you are a lamp! Hop over creaky boards and the robot vacuum, hide behind plants when your nosy brother shines his flashlight, and dodge mom on her late night water run. Grab pillows, teddies and snacks on the way to the front door. There are 100 levels in 5 worlds with a boss level every 10 levels.

  • 100 levels in 5 cozy worlds, each with a new trick
  • Hold to tiptoe, freeze, hop and hide
  • Funny snoring family, cute and never scary
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
