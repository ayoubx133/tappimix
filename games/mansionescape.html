Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Swipe a cute bow tie ghost through a silly mansion, grab the key and glide out!

About the game

Swipe to make the little ghost glide until it bonks into furniture, then plan your path to the golden key and the mansion door. The sleepy butler sleepwalks one step every time you glide, so never stop in his candle light or he tucks you back in the cupboard. Pull levers to move secret bookcases, bounce off dancing bedsheet ghosts, jump into magic paintings and stick to jam puddles. There are 100 levels in 5 worlds with a hint bulb whenever you need help.

  • 100 glide puzzles in 5 silly mansion worlds
  • Secret bookcases, bouncy ghosts and magic paintings
  • Cute ghost and a sleepy butler, never scary
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
