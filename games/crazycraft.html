Crazy Block House Craft

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Build wacky block mansions and watch silly visitors try every room!

About the game

Each visitor shows you their wishes: a pool, a slide, a disco, a ball pit, a candy room and lots more. Pick a room card, tap an empty spot and watch the blocks rain down to build it. Ring the doorbell and the visitor tours your house, splashing, bouncing, sliding and dancing through every room, then gives you up to 3 stars. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds with new twists like painting the house, crazy roof toppers, two visitors at once and room combos.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds with party levels
  • 16 wacky rooms with funny visitor reactions
  • Paint, roof toppers, combos and 12 silly visitors
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
