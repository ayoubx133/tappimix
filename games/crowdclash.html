Crowd Cannon Clash

FamilyFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Fire a jelly crowd through x gates and splat the grumpy goo fort!

About the game

Hold your finger down to launch a stream of jelly blobs and drag left or right to aim the cannon. Send the crowd through glowing x2, x3 and x4 gates to multiply it, then pour into the goo fort until its number hits zero. Sweep your crowd across the track to stop the grumpy goo blobs before they reach your shield. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds with moving gates, spinning candy bars, giant goo blobs and sliding shield walls.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds, each with a new twist
  • Multiply your jelly crowd through glowing gates
  • Upgrade cannon power and unlock 6 jelly skins
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
