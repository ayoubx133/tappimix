About the game

Tap to build your own floating island in the sky. Plant palms and flowers, dig ponds, raise new land and decorate with houses, lamps and windmills. Cute animals parachute in when your island has what they love, and every one of them reacts when you pet it. Finish 60 island goals and fresh daily tasks while the sun sets and the stars come out.

  • Grow your island from 9 to 81 blocks
  • 10 animal friends with day and night
  • 60 island goals plus daily tasks
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
