About the game

Pick a smiling jelly planet and play with it using 15 silly tools: poke it, tickle it with a giant feather, blow bubbles, flick jelly rocks, paint it, freeze it and warm it up again. Hold the rainbow beam to crack a planet into glowing pieces and watch it snap back together with a giggle. Clear 100 poke challenges in 5 galaxies by lighting up the star spots before the timer runs out, or relax in free play with every tool. Unlock new planets and collect fun lab gloves as you go.

  • 20 squishy planets and 15 silly tools
  • 100 poke challenges in 5 galaxies
  • Planets wobble, crack and always re-form
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
