About the game

Race, kick, bump, jump and dance with four bouncy stickmen in 30 funny one button mini games. Play alone against cheeky computer friends and win your way through 100 levels in 5 worlds: Race Park, Ball Stadium, Tank Arena, Snake Garden and Party Castle. Every 10th level is a Cup Final with three games in a row and a trophy podium, and surprise rules like giant heads, turbo speed and disco lights pop up along the way. In Party mode, 2 to 4 friends share one tablet and each tap their own big corner pad.

  • 30 one button mini games and 100 levels in 5 worlds
  • Party mode for 2 to 4 players on one tablet
  • Cup Finals, surprise rules and 6 stickman styles
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
