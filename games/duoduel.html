About the game

Pick one of 14 mini games and play against a friend on the same tablet: RED plays on the left half and BLUE on the right half. Race chickens by tapping fast, fly rockets without overheating, bump each other out of the ring, score soccer goals, pop balloons, catch fruit, answer math questions and much more. Every duel is short, so you can play again and again and count your wins. No friend around? Switch to VS ROBOT and play against the computer.

  • 14 different two player games on one screen
  • Play with a friend or against a friendly robot
  • Unlock 6 cute player teams with your coins
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
