About the game

Build your own blocky club room: place dance floor tiles, sofas, speakers, a DJ booth, arcade machines, lights and more, then change the wall and floor colors. Invite blocky friends who walk around, dance, sit, snack and play, and tap Party to start a dance party. Play three mini games, Dance Battle, Block Stack and DJ Pads, to earn coins for new items and cool hoodies. Raise your club hype to level up, unlock more friends and finish quests and daily tasks.

  • Decorate a blocky club with 16 items, dance floors and colors
  • Invite up to 8 friends who dance, snack and play
  • 3 mini games, 50 quests, daily tasks and 6 hoodies
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
