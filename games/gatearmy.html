About the game

Drag left and right to lead your brave princess and her toy soldiers through the gates with the best numbers, like +10 or x2, and stay away from the minus gates. Dodge rolling foam logs, swinging pillows and flying pillows, then win silly pillow fights against the grumpy red army. At the end, charge the castle and tap tap tap to bonk the grumpy gate with the giant foam hammer. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds with a big boss every 10 levels.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds with a boss every 10 levels
  • Grow a huge toy army and win pillow fights
  • 6 army colours to unlock
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
