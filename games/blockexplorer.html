About the game

Tap to walk, tap blocks near you to gather wood, stone, berries and wool, then pick a block in the hotbar and tap to build. Craft planks, pickaxes, torches, beds, campfires and bounce pads. When night falls, silly goo blobs bounce around: build a cozy shelter or tap them to pop them. Play 100 adventure quests in 5 biomes: meadow hills, lake forest, sunny desert, snowy peaks and mushroom isles.

  • 100 adventure quests in 5 biomes with day and night
  • Gather, craft and build shelters, bridges and campfires
  • Friendly animals and silly goo blobs that pop
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
