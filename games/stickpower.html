About the game

Drag the green stick hero onto a tower room with a smaller number. A silly foam pillow duel follows, the foe poofs into confetti and your hero grabs its power and grows bigger. Pick the right order, drink power potions, double up with x2 orbs, dodge banana peels and goo, and find keys for locked doors. Beat a giant boss every 10th level across 100 levels in 5 worlds.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds with a new twist in every world
  • Funny foam pillow duels, burps, sneezes and confetti poofs
  • 6 hero outfits to unlock: ninja, super, cowboy, astro and king
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
