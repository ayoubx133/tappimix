Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Build walls and ramps in a tap, foam the wind-up toys and beat the storm!

About the game

Drag anywhere to run while your foam dart blaster auto aims at the nearest wind-up toy. Bonk trees with your toy hammer for wood, then tap WALL for instant cover, RAMP for high ground and FORT to box yourself in a cozy fort. Open chests for better blasters, stay ahead of the purple storm circle and finish in the top three to win. Play 100 matches on 5 maps and unlock heroes, dances and coins on the Fort Pass.

  • 100 matches on 5 maps with a new twist on each
  • Tap to build walls, ramps and instant forts
  • Fort Pass with 20 tiers, 6 heroes and 6 dances
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
