About the game

The cheeky baby crawls to the cookie jar, the toilet paper and the paint pots, so tap him before his prank is done and the big babysitter hand scoops him back into the playpen. Scrub away the funny messes, drag the bottle over when he gets hungry, find him when he hides and catch him during a silly sugar rush. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds, from the nursery and the kitchen to the bathroom, the living room and the garden at night, with a flying super baby every tenth night. Earn stars and coins and dress the baby in cute outfits.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds, from the nursery to the garden

  • 6 cute outfits for the baby, stars and coins to earn
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
