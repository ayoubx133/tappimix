About the game

Ruby dashes down the train yard while the grumpy yard keeper and his puppy chase her. Swipe left and right to switch tracks, swipe up to jump over barriers and swipe down to roll under signs. Dodge parked and rolling trains, run up ramps onto the train roofs, bounce on giant springs and grab a hoverboard that saves you from one bump. Collect coins along the way to earn stars and unlock new runners.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds, from a sunny yard to a night express
  • Simple swipe controls with ramps, springs and hoverboards
  • Unlock 6 runners with their own colorful outfits
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
