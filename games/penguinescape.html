Baby Penguin Jail Escape

FamilyFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Help a giggly baby in a penguin onesie belly slide out of a silly toy jail!

About the game

Swipe to make the baby belly slide across the icy floor until something stops the slide, then find the path to the open door. Wait until the sleepy guard snores before you slide past him, grab a squeaky ducky to keep the awake guard busy, collect gold keys, bounce on pillows and whoosh down laundry chutes. Every tenth level the hungry chief guard blocks the door, so slide over three donuts to give him a big food nap. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds, earn stars and coins and dress the baby in cute onesies.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds with a new trick in every world
  • Funny sleepy guards, squeaky toys, pillows, keys and chutes
  • 6 cute onesies, stars, coins and a hint button for little players
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
