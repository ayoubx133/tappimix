Sneaky Granny Escape House

FamilyFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Sneak out of grumpy granny's house, find the keys and hide in closets!

About the game

Tap the house to tiptoe from room to room and search drawers for keys and secret notes. Stay out of granny's candle light, slip into a closet when she comes near, and use the stairs to dodge her. Crack the padlock, crawl through vents and watch out for her sleepy cat. If she spots you, she just shoos you back to bed with her broom, so try again! There are 100 levels in 5 worlds to explore.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds, each with a new trick
  • Find keys, crack padlocks and hide in closets
  • Funny grumpy granny, cute and never scary
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
