Dream Life Story World

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Live funny stories with Leo and friends in a little city full of surprises!

About the game

Follow 11 story chapters with Leo and his friends: wake him up, flip pancakes that land on heads, go to school, visit the doctor, shop at the mall, get crazy haircuts and play in the park. Fly between places with Leo's red balloon on the city map and drag friends into beds, desks, doctor beds, salon chairs, swings and the trampoline, where every spot does something silly. Give anyone a balloon to float up to the ceiling, then pop it for a big bounce, switch day and night and throw a dance party. Finish story steps and daily tasks to unlock new places, earn coins and fill your sticker book.

  • 11 story chapters with 55 fun steps to play
  • 6 places to explore by flying with a balloon
  • Drag friends anywhere, daily tasks and a sticker book
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
