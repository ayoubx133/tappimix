King Jumbo Blast

FamilyFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Blast squishy jelly blobs and help jolly King Jumbo rebuild his castle town!

About the game

Tap a group of 2 or more touching jelly blobs of the same color to pop them and reach the level goals before the moves run out. Pop 5 or more at once for a royal trumpet, 7 or more for a cake bomb and 9 or more for a rainbow crown, then tap boosters side by side for a giant combo. Every win adds a new building to King Jumbo's town, from the Market Square to the Throne Castle. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds with wooden crates, growing frosting, cooing pigeons, floating bubbles, treasure chests and a grumpy cloud boss.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds with a new challenge in every world
  • Rebuild 100 buildings in King Jumbo's castle town
  • Trumpet, cake bomb and rainbow crown combos
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
