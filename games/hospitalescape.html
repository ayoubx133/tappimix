Kitty Hospital Escape

FamilyFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Run, jump and slide a brave kitten out of the hospital before the goo hugs you!

About the game

A bandaged little kitten runs down the hospital halls while a goofy purple goo blob slurps after her. Swipe left and right to change lanes, swipe up to jump over boxes and wet floor signs, and swipe down to slide under beds. Grab keycards to open the right door, hide in laundry baskets when the goo lunges, and ride skateboards to zoom ahead. 100 levels in 5 worlds, from the wobbly ward to the rooftop garden!

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds with new tricks in each
  • A silly goo blob that slurps, lunges and gets washed
  • Easy swipe or tap controls for kids
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
