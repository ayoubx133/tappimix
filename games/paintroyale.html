Parachute onto paint island, splat the jelly buddies, be the last one standing!

About the game

Jump from the blimp, glide down on your parachute and grab paint sprayers, bubble shields and paint refills. Drag anywhere to move while your sprayer splashes the nearest rival automatically, and splatted jelly buddies pop back to the lobby with a giggle. Stay inside the safe zone because the purple paint storm keeps closing in, then finish in the top three to win the match. Play 100 matches on 5 maps with water balloons, hiding bushes, supply drops and bounce pads, and unlock rewards on the Paint Pass.

  • 100 matches on 5 colorful maps
  • Paint sprayers, water balloons and a shrinking safe zone
  • Paint Pass with 20 reward tiers and 6 paint kids
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
