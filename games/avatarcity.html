Make your own avatar, dress up and explore a cute city with pets!

About the game

Create your avatar with lots of faces, hairstyles, colors, outfits and fun extras, then walk down the city street. Visit your room, the cafe, the mall, the school and the star studio, order treats, play the piano, take photos and strike poses on stage. Decorate every room with stickers and bring cute pets along. Finish quests and daily tasks to earn coins, buy new clothes, unlock pets and sticker packs and fill your sticker book.

  • Avatar maker with faces, 10 hairstyles, outfits, shoes and extras
  • Explore 5 city places with stickers, pets, photos, piano and a stage
  • 60 quests, daily tasks, coins to spend and pets to unlock
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
