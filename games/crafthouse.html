Craft House Builder

FamilyFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Build block houses from blueprints, furnish every room and make villagers happy.

About the game

Pick a block and drag your finger over the blueprint to build each house block by block. When the house stands, the front wall opens and you drag beds, lamps, sofas, stoves and plants into the rooms, then add a garden. The villager moves in and rates your work with hearts and stars. Build 100 houses in 5 worlds: meadow cottages, beach stilt houses, snowy log cabins, a desert town with mirror building and sky castles you paint yourself.

  • 100 houses in 5 worlds with new blocks and tools in each
  • Build from blueprints, then furnish every room your way
  • Cute villagers move in and rate your house with stars
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
