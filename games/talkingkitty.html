My Talking Kitty Star

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Care for a cute kitty who talks, sings and giggles when you tap her!

About the game

Look after your own fluffy kitty: feed her yummy treats, scrub her with soap bubbles in the bath, tuck her into bed and dress her up in hats, outfits and glasses. Tap her head, nose, tummy and tail to hear her purr, sneeze, giggle and meow, and tap Talk or Sing to hear her cute voice, no microphone needed. Play Sing Along, Treat Catch and Bubble Pop to earn coins, then decorate her home with new walls, floors, sofas and more. Finish quests and daily tasks to collect stickers and new fur styles.

  • Feed, bathe, dress up and put your kitty to bed
  • She talks, sings and reacts to every tap, no microphone
  • 3 mini games, home decor, 60 quests and 6 fur styles
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
