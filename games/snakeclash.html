Steer a jelly snake, eat candy dots, grow big and bonk smaller snakes!

About the game

Hold and drag anywhere to steer your cute jelly snake around the arena. Eat glowing candy dots to grow longer, and when your head bumps a smaller snake it goes BONK and poofs into a pile of candy. Watch out for bigger snakes, beat a giant crowned boss every 10th level, and enjoy 100 levels in 5 worlds with speed pads, magnets, golden apples, slippy ice and portals. Earn coins to unlock fun snake skins with hats and patterns.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds with a boss every 10th level
  • Silly snakes that hiccup, burp, giggle and go dizzy
  • 6 snake skins with stripes, stars, rainbows and crowns
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
