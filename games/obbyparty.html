About the game

Your smiley cube buddy runs across floating block courses high in the sky. Drag left and right to steer and tap to jump over gaps, onto sliding candy platforms and past spinning foam bars. Steer around purple goo, bounce on springy pads and time your hops on blinking ice blocks. Fall off and you pop right back at the last checkpoint, so every course can be finished.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds, each with a brand new obstacle
  • Easy drag to steer and tap to jump controls with checkpoints
  • Unlock 6 cube buddies with hats and colorful outfits
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
