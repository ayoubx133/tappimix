Fling, bounce and tickle wobbly veggie friends in a silly science lab sandbox!

About the game

Play with Zucchini and his floppy veggie friends Carrot, Tomato, Pepper, Broccoli, Corn and Eggplant in a cozy science lab. Drag and fling them onto trampolines, tie ropes, freeze them in the air and tickle them with soft lab toys like the fizz beaker, soup pot and watering can. Finish 50 fun lab quests to win stickers and open new places, from the greenhouse to the moon farm. Nobody ever gets hurt: veggies just wobble, giggle and pop right back up.

  • 7 floppy veggie friends and 30 soft toys
  • 50 lab quests, stickers and 5 places to unlock
  • Ropes, pins, freeze, slow motion and moon gravity
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
