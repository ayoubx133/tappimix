Ragdoll Ninja Duel

FamilyFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Hold, let go, flip and bonk! One button ninja duels for 1 or 2 players.

About the game

Hold anywhere to crouch and charge, then let go to leap and spin your glowing foam staff. Bonk the other ninja on the head so their headband flies off, or bounce them off the roof, and the first to 3 bonks wins. Fight 100 duels in 5 worlds: Moon Rooftops, Bamboo Bridge, Lantern Festival, Windy Peaks and Cherry Temple, each with a new twist like bridge gaps, bouncy lanterns and wind. Every 10th duel is a big boss, and in 2 player mode two friends share one tablet, one side each.

  • 100 duels in 5 worlds with gaps, bouncy lanterns and wind
  • 2 player mode on one tablet plus silly CPU ninjas
  • 6 ninja styles, boss duels and surprise rules
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
