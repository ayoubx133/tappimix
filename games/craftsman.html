About the game

Tap the glowing spots to stack blocks and copy each blueprint before the clock runs out. When the last block lands, your statue opens its googly eyes, talks and dances. Pick the right colors, pop the hungry goo blobs that nibble your blocks and copy secret blueprint cards in snowy peaks. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds with a giant build every 10 levels.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds with giant builds
  • Silly statues that come alive and talk
  • 6 builders to unlock with your coins
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
