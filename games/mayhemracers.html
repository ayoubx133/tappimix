About the game

Drag your finger toward where you want to go and your car drives that way; drag far to speed up and pull back to slow down for corners. Win races around the city blocks against rival cars, pop balloons in bumper car derbies and fly off stunt ramps to grab every star. Watch out for water gaps, slippy puddles and icy roads, and hit the boost pads. Play 100 events in 5 districts and unlock 6 fun cars.

  • 100 events in 5 districts: races, bumper car derbies and stunt shows
  • Ramps, boost pads, star rings, puddles and icy roads
  • Friendly cartoon chaos with no crime, just racing fun
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
