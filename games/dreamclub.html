About the game

Run your own soccer club: train your squad, sign better players from the transfer market and upgrade your stadium from a park pitch to a glowing dream arena. In matches, tap a teammate to pass, swipe to shoot and tap a rival to press and win the ball back. Curve your shots, splash through rainy puddles and charge super shots as you climb 100 matches in 5 divisions. Earn ticket coins every win and unlock 6 club kits.

  • 100 matches in 5 divisions to climb
  • Sign, train and upgrade your club and stadium
  • Simple tap to pass and swipe to shoot controls
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
