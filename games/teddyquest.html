About the game

Brave Teddy runs all by himself, you just tap to jump and hold to jump higher. Stomp the giggly goo blobs, grab shiny buttons and reach the star flag at the end of every level. Explore 100 levels in 5 worlds: Button Meadow, Waterfall Cliffs, Mushroom Woods, Snowy Peaks and Candy Cloud Castle, each with a new trick like spin jumps, bouncy mushrooms, slippy ice and wind fans. Every 10th level the silly Goo King waits in his arena, so bounce on his crown three times to win!

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds with a new trick in every world
  • Silly goo blobs, bees, snowballs and a Goo King boss
  • 6 cute teddies to collect with buttons
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
