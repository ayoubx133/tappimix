Run the runway, pick style gates and wow the silly animal judges!

About the game

Drag left and right to steer your doll down the runway and run through the gates that match the theme of the level, like Princess, Sporty, Rock Star or Beach Fun. Every gate changes her hair, dress, shoes, hat or bag right away, but watch out for the silly gates with mop wigs, fish hats and banana shoes. Dodge mud puddles, grab hearts, then strut the catwalk, strike a pose and get score cards from three funny animal judges. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds and collect 6 dolls.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds with new gate tricks
  • Silly outfits, funny judges and catwalk poses
  • 6 dolls to collect and style
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
