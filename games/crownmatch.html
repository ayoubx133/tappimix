About the game

Swap two gems next to each other to make a line of 3 or more and clear the level goals before you run out of moves. Match 4 or 5 gems to make rockets, bombs, propellers and rainbow orbs, then swap two of them together for giant combos. Every win gives a royal star that the young king uses to rebuild his castle, from the Throne Hall to the Grand Ballroom. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds with crates, ice, sticky honey, golden crowns and spreading jam.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds with a new challenge in every world
  • Rebuild 5 castle rooms with the stars you win
  • Big combos with rockets, bombs and rainbow orbs
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
