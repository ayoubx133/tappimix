About the game

Tap a block and your little miner walks through the tunnels and digs it out. Collect wood, stone, coal, copper, iron, crystals, gold and star gems, then craft stronger pickaxes, torches and treasures at the crafting table. Watch your energy, grab apple crates for a boost and finish every mine goal. Explore 100 mines in 5 worlds with falling sand, dark crystal caves, fizzy pop blocks and hot embers you cool with a water bucket.

  • 100 mines in 5 worlds with new blocks and tricks in each
  • Crafting table with pickaxes, torches, buckets and treasures
  • Friendly critters: bunnies, glow bugs, moles and rock buddies
  • Simple one-tap controls for all ages
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
